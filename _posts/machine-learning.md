## Hypernetworks

Networks that act as meta networks or meta models and create other models by editing/designing their weights.
A review can be found here: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.06955v3>
Apparently this is nothing new and not ground breaking, but recent LLMs have gotten quite good at it.
